FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question that isn't listed here? Our architects are happy to help.

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does house design cost?
Design fees depend on plot size, number of floors and the scope you choose: floor plans only, plans with 3D elevation, or complete architecture with interiors. Book a free consultation and we'll share a clear, itemised quote for your project.
Do you provide turnkey solutions?
Yes. Our turnkey service covers design, approvals support, construction, interiors and final handover, with one team, one contract and one point of contact.
Can you handle construction projects?
Absolutely. We manage complete construction for residential and commercial buildings, with on-site supervision, quality checks and regular progress reports.
Do you provide 3D elevation designs?
Yes. We create realistic 3D front elevations and interior visualisations so you can see and refine your home before construction begins.
Do you work outside Patna?
Yes. Alongside Patna, Danapur and Khagaul, we take on projects across Bihar. Design services can also be delivered remotely with scheduled site visits.
